## You may

- Use the name "Twoody" to truthfully refer to the upstream project,

- Distribute unmodified binary releases that retain the "Twoody" name.
- Mention "Twoody" in package metadata when packaging the upstream source
  for a Linux distribution, Homebrew, or similar repositories, as long as the

- Use the "Twoody" name, logo, or any confusingly similar mark for a
  modified fork, a competing product, or a hosted service, without prior

- Imply official endorsement, partnership, or affiliation with Osmove that

- Register a domain, organization, or company name that includes "Twoody"
  or a confusingly similar variant.

## Forks

If you publish a fork that diverges meaningfully from upstream, please
rename it. We suggest a name that does not contain "Twoody" or a
confusingly similar string. You can still describe the fork as
"based on Twoody" or "a fork of Twoody" — that's truthful nominative use.

## Questions

For any trademark question — commercial use, packaging, or fork naming —
email **trademark@twoody.com**. We try to be reasonable; the goal of this
policy is to keep the "Twoody" name a reliable signal of the upstream
project, not to discourage downstream use.
